# Milestone 4: knights vs orcs, levels 51-100 and endless

What changed after M3 feedback (the game was played through to 50):

- new models
- keyboard Brute
- less shake under heavy fire
- more levels

## What shipped

### Art: fantasy knights vs orcs

Every unit is a new low-poly model built from merged primitives with vertex colours (`src/render/visuals.ts`). It is
still one draw call per unit type.

| unit | model | triangles |
|---|---|---|
| Soldier | knight: steel helm, blue tabard and cape, spear | 122 |
| Runner | scout: light helm, blue hood, dagger | 70 |
| Brute | paladin: plate, gold trim, tower shield, warhammer | 220 |
| Grunt | orc: green skin, tusks, red eyes, cleaver | 122 |
| Shield | orc shield-bearer: iron helm, wooden round shield | 142 |
| Swarm | goblin: big ears, small blade | 92 |
| Ogre | grey-blue troll with a club | 144 |
| Warden (boss) | orc warlord: horned helm, bone pauldrons, great axe | ~360 |
| Tyrant (boss) | troll king: crown, cape, spiked maul | ~374 |

- **Walk cycle in the vertex shader.**
  - Each vertex carries a walk group (left/right limb) and a hip height. The CPU still writes 4 floats per unit, and
    the y slot now holds a walk phase instead of a constant.
  - The shader swings legs and arms in opposite phase, sways the body and bobs it twice per step.
  - Stride, swing and bob are per type: goblins scurry and trolls lumber.
- **Orc stronghold** replaces the castle:
  - rock footing and a log palisade with sharpened tips
  - a gate with an iron-banded door and gate towers
  - watchtowers on stilts
  - red banners, skull poles and iron spikes

  The static parts are one merged mesh. Roofs, banners, skulls and spikes still break off as the base takes damage.
- **Launcher:** a bronze cannon on a stone emplacement.
- Enemy hit and death colours follow the orc palette.
- **Dev gallery:**
  - `npm run dev`, then `/gallery.html` shows every model turning slowly; `?type=boss` shows one on its own.
  - `node scripts/gallery-shot.mjs <url> <out.png> <w> <h>` screenshots it.
  - Not in the production build.

### Controls and feel

- **B sends the Brute** on PC (E and Shift still work). The Brute button shows a `B` key hint on devices with a
  mouse; phones don't show it.
- **Shake under heavy fire:**
  - Base and fortress hits now add a capped "rumble". It can never push the camera's shake trauma past 0.15, so a
    big army hitting the castle every tick gives a steady low rumble instead of violent shaking.
  - Big one-off events (boss stomps, base destroyed) still use the full shake.

### Levels 51-100 and endless

- **Levels 51-100** come from the same seeded generator. Five gate rows from level 60.
- **Bosses:**

  | level | name | line-up |
  |---|---|---|
  | 60 | Warlord's Pact | Tyrant + Warden |
  | 70 | Twin Kings | 2 Tyrants |
  | 80 | War Council | 3 Wardens |
  | 90 | The Horde | Tyrant + 2 Wardens |
  | 100 | Final Siege | 2 Tyrants + 2 Wardens |

- **Escorts:** levels ending in 5 bring a Warden from 25 and a Tyrant from 75.
- **Intro cards** at 51, 70, 75, 100 and 101.
- **Lateness and level length:**
  - Lateness keeps rising to level 71 and is capped there (`LATE_CAP`).
  - Target length tops out at 60 s (+10 on bosses).
- **Endless mode (101+):**
  - Unlocks after beating 100, from an ∞ tile on the level map that shows your best endless level.
  - Each endless level is generated on demand from its number (same number, same level).
  - **101-160 are calibrated per level** against maxed upgrades. The margin climbs 2% of the bot's ceiling per
    level and passes 100% around level 110, so endless is a wall that arrives steadily: how far you get is the score.
    Better weapons are the player's edge, since the bot only uses the Blaster.
  - **Past 160** strength and base extrapolate from the last calibrated block, growing 3% a level.
  - **Gate values** (tolls, + gates, flip thresholds) also grow 3% a level from 100. Before this, they followed the
    campaign curve (×1.075 a level), and by level 135 a single toll took ~26k units and wiped out any army.
  - Boss every 10 (Tyrant + Warden).
- Levels 16-50 are unchanged: a test pins them by hash, so the generator changes cannot shift levels people have
  already played.
- `npm run balance -- <from> <to> --calibrate` now calibrates a range and keeps the other entries.

## Results

`npm run balance` over 1-100: **0 unwinnable, 0 soft.**

- **51-100:** the skilled bot with the expected upgrades wins every level on every seed in 34-64 s. The same
  perfect-aim bot without upgrades loses every one of them.
- **Expected upgrades:** 6-8 of 10 per track at 51, and Fire Rate, Volley, Gate Power, Reserves and Siege maxed
  by about 88.
- **Strength vs upgrades:** once upgrades max out, calibrated enemy strength (k) falls toward 0.01 by level 100,
  because the difficulty curve D keeps growing ×1.075 a level. The calibrator's floor was lowered from 0.05 to
  1e-5; at 0.05, levels 79, 90 and 97-100 had come out broken.

**Endless, skilled bot, every upgrade maxed, Blaster** (`scripts/endless-check.ts`):

| levels | result |
|---|---|
| 101-106 | all won, 45-66 s |
| 107-110 | first losses (1 seed on 107) |
| 111-122 | about half lost: the wall |

Better weapons push past where the bot stops.

## Performance

`npm run bench:browser` on the Intel Arc 140T, stress scene with ~5,400 units drawn in 56 draw calls:

| profile | fps | GPU ms (mean) |
|---|---|---|
| desktop, uncapped | 458 | 2.1 |
| phone (4× CPU throttle) | 126 | 4.6 |
| phone, 60 Hz vsync | 53 (vsync-bound) | 5.1 |

The new models (70-374 triangles each, against 64-88 before) and the shader walk cycle did not cost a measurable
frame budget. The previous saved run on this machine was 6.4 ms GPU, but it predates later optimisations, so read
this as no regression rather than a speed-up.

## Known issues / next

- **Endless tuning knobs.** If endless ramps too fast or too slow, change `ENDLESS_RAMP` (calibrated 101-160) or
  `ENDLESS_GROWTH` (past 160) in `generator.ts`. Then rerun `npm run balance -- 101 160 --calibrate`.
- **Late base HP swings a lot between neighbouring levels.** This is because the gate layout decides how big the
  army gets. The cause is the same as in M3, but the numbers are larger with five gate rows.
- **Next (feel):** audio, slow-mo moments, win celebration.
