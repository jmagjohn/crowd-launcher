# Milestone 3: levels 16-50, a harder late game

Scope follows the revised roadmap (docs/PLAN.md). Levels 1-15, the economy and mobile were left as they were.
Player-simulation bands were dropped; the one automated check left is "every level is winnable with the upgrades
a player would own by then".

## What shipped

- **35 new levels (16-50)** from a seeded generator (`src/levels/generator.ts`). The same level number always
  gives the same level; there is a test for that.
  - **Gate rows** are built from seven templates:
    - ×a vs a narrow ×(a+1)
    - a toll guarding a ×4/×5
    - + vs ×2
    - a ÷ between edge ×3s
    - a flip gate
    - a moving ×3
    - a limited-use ×5

    Three rows up to level 24, four after. Late levels get narrower doors, ÷3 instead of ÷2, faster moving gates,
    bigger tolls and earlier flips.
  - **Waves:** 4 waves early, 6 late. Each mixes two enemy types more often as levels climb. Waves spread over the
    level's target length.
  - **Bosses and breathers:**
    - Level 20: Warden's Return
    - Level 30: the Tyrant
    - Level 40: Twin Wardens (staggered)
    - Level 50: Tyrant + Warden
    - From level 25, levels ending in 5 bring an escorting Warden.
    - The level after each boss is a breather.
  - **Intro cards** at 16 (Ogres), 25 (escorts), 30 (Tyrant) and 40 (two bosses). Rewards grow +7 coins per level,
    and boss levels pay 1.6×.
- **New enemies:**
  - **Ogre** (from level 16): 12 HP, slow, a green brawler. A thin stream just feeds it.
  - **Tyrant** (levels 30 and 50): a purple boss with the Warden's stomp cycle. Every stomp also summons 16 grunts
    in front of it (scaled by dynamic difficulty).
- **Level map** holds all 50 levels (scrolls, opens centred on your current level).

## How difficulty is set

Random gate layouts change how hard a level is by up to 4×, so one formula could not hit a target. Each generated
level is calibrated against the sim instead (`npm run balance -- --calibrate`, about 5 minutes):

1. **Expected upgrades for level L:** coins from first clears of 1..L-1, spent on the cheapest upgrade each time.
   It assumes the Blaster and no replays (a conservative player).
2. **Ceiling:** the strongest waves the skilled bot with those upgrades survives for the level's target length.
   The target length is 30 s at level 16, rising to 55 s at 50 (+10 s on bosses).
3. **Enemy strength:** the level is set at a margin of that ceiling. The margin rises from about 50% at level 16 to
   82% in the 40s, with the sawtooth: bosses tighter, the level after a boss looser.
4. **Base HP:** 90% of the damage that bot deals in the target time. Late castles have 20k-100k HP.
5. **Back-off:** if any seed fails, strength backs off 8% at a time until every seed wins.

Results are stored in `src/levels/tuning.ts` (generated, committed). `npm run balance` re-checks all 50 levels and
fails if any is unwinnable.

## Results (`npm run balance`)

| levels | skilled bot + expected upgrades | skilled bot, no upgrades |
|---|---|---|
| 1-15 | wins all | wins all |
| 16-20 | wins all, 19-35 s | loses 5 of 5 |
| 21-29 | wins all, 28-34 s | loses 7 of 9 (21 and 22 still winnable) |
| 30-39 | wins all, 33-43 s | loses all |
| 40-50 | wins all, 39-52 s | loses all |

The "no upgrades" column is a perfect-aim bot. From level 23 on, aim alone is not enough.

**Sanity sample (not a tuning target):** an "average" bot (noisy aim, slow reactions) with the same conservative
upgrades. It wins 6/6 on 16-25 most of the time, about 2-5 of 6 on most of 30-50, and 0/6 on level 28. Real players
have three extra slack sources: better weapons, replay coins, and the −15% assist after repeated losses.

Expected upgrade levels at 50 are 6-8 of 10 per track, so the caps are not hit and were left alone.

## Tests (145 passing)

- **Generator:** 50 unique ids; determinism; a calibration entry per level; gates stay in the lane and never
  overlap; bosses on 20/30/40/50 with a breather after; late enemy HP is more than 2.5× early and base HP more than
  1.5× early; rewards rise.
- **Tyrant:** each stomp summons exactly 16 grunts in front of it; the summon scales with dynamic difficulty.
- **Determinism and per-tick unit conservation** now run on all 50 levels.

## Known issues / next

- **Calibration is against bots, not people.** If late levels feel unfair in practice, the single knob is
  `targetMargin` in `generator.ts`; lower it, then rerun `npm run balance -- --calibrate`.
- **Sim changes need recalibration.** Any change to the sim, gates or generator changes the levels, so rerun
  calibrate afterwards; `npm run balance` flags it otherwise.
- **M4 (feel):** audio, slow-mo moments, win celebration, camera follow.
